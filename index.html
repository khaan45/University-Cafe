Departments

Each department keeps its own income, expenses, and purchase orders. Click a card to see its full statement below.
Department Names
Renaming a department relabels every entry, bill, and purchase order already recorded under its old name — nothing is lost.

Bank Accounts

Each bank account becomes its own Asset account in the ledger — it'll show up as an option anywhere you can currently pick "Cash" (New Entry, invoice/bill payments, payroll). It'll also show up below, in All Accounts, alongside every other account — that's the one place to edit, remove, or drill into any of them.
Transfer Between Accounts
Transfers between Cash/Bank accounts are internal — they won't show up as Operating/Investing/Financing activity on the Cash Flow Statement.
All Accounts
The full chart of accounts — Cash, bank accounts, and wallets are editable/removable here; everything else (receivables/payables, equity, revenue, expense) is click-through only. Every balance is all-time.

Pay All Outstanding Bills

This marks every outstanding bill as fully paid in one go and posts a single journal entry for the total — only use this if you've actually paid all of it (e.g. reconciling a batch of vendor payments at once). Individual bills can still be paid one at a time from the list below instead.

Receive All Outstanding Invoices

This marks every outstanding invoice as fully received in one go and posts a single journal entry for the total — only use this if you've actually collected all of it (e.g. reconciling a batch of payments at once). Individual invoices can still be received one at a time from the list below instead.

⚠ Set Same Date for ALL Invoices

This overwrites the Invoice Date and Due Date on every single invoice — including ones that already have the correct date. It does not just fix broken ones. This can't be undone in bulk. Only use this if every invoice genuinely needs to share the same date (e.g. a one-off batch of invoices that should all be dated the same day).

Restore from Backup

This replaces everything currently on this device — every customer, invoice, bill, journal entry, and setting — with what's in the backup file. Anything entered since that backup was made will be lost. This can't be undone.

Clear Data

This permanently deletes data from this device/account's storage. It can't be undone. Logins (users) are never touched here — use Manage Users for that.
